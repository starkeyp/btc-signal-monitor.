How to read this board

SMA 20 / SMA 50 are average closes over 20 and 50 candles. Price above both usually means an uptrend; below both a downtrend. The 48h bars show whether those averages are rising or falling.

RSI 14 is momentum on a 0–100 scale. Below 30 is oversold (bounce risk up). Above 70 is overbought (fade risk down). Mid 40–60 is neutral.

MACD histogram is trend acceleration. Green / positive = upside pressure building. Red / negative = downside pressure building. A flip through zero is a momentum change.

Signal rules

BUY if price is above SMA50 and RSI is under 35, or price reclaims SMA20 with RSI 35–55 and a rising MACD histogram.

SELL if RSI is over 72 and price is under SMA20, or price is under SMA50 with a falling MACD histogram and RSI still above 55.

HOLD otherwise. 1h / 4h / 1d only changes which candles the signal uses. Educational only — this page does not place trades.
